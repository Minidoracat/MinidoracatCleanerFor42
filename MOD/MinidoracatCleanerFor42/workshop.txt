description=[h2]📦 Requirements[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] Required: [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3789836701]Minidoracat UI Library for B42[/url], on both server and clients
description=[*] Build 42 only, singleplayer and multiplayer; servers must enable it
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]🚀 Quick start[/h2]
description=[olist]
description=[*] Select items in your inventory or a nearby container, right-click "Delete" and confirm
description=[*] Auto-cleanup works out of the box and warns nearby players first
description=[*] Admins can tune the limits in sandbox settings or switch auto-cleanup off
description=[*] Right-click the ground to open the list manager and add items or animals
description=[/olist]
description=
description=[h2]✨ Features[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] [b]Delete from any container[/b]: backpacks, cabinets, trunks, the floor
description=[*] [b]Ground piles auto-cleaned[/b]: warning first, newest pile goes first
description=[*] [b]Junk vs. scenery[/b]: natural forest branches and grass are left alone
description=[*] [b]Animal limits[/b]: near players, server-wide and per farm
description=[*] [b]Farm-safe[/b]: penned animals are left alone; can stop breeding without killing
description=[*] [b]See who did it[/b]: who dropped an item, who placed furniture
description=[*] [b]Cleanup log[/b]: every warning and cleanup is recorded
description=[/list]
