description=[h2]✨ What is this?[/h2]
description=Sanitation service for the apocalypse — for "junk everywhere on the floor" and "animals bred until the server chokes". Three things:
description=[list]
description=[*] [b]Delete items from any container[/b] — backpacks, cabinets, trunks, the floor; right-click and it's gone, no more garbage-can-only
description=[*] [b]Auto-clean duplicate items piling up on the ground[/b] — someone dumped 500 bullets? Warn first, clean only if still over the limit on the next scan
description=[*] [b]Auto-remove overbred animals[/b] — runaway rats, rabbits and chickens are a known B42 performance killer
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]⚠️ Requirement[/h2]
description=Requires the author's [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3789836701]Minidoracat UI Library for B42[/url] (listed as a required item). Install on both server and clients, and place it before this mod in the server's Mods list.
description=
description=[h2]🧰 Features[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] [b]Protected deletion[/b]: favorites, equipped/worn items and key rings are never deleted; confirmation dialog; in multiplayer the server validates, so regular players need no admin rights
description=[*] [b]Tells junk from scenery[/b]: natural forest-floor branches, logs and bushes are not treated as player dumping — they get a high-tolerance limit and won't be cleaned by mistake
description=[*] [b]Newest piles go first[/b]: items that were legitimately there are kept; items enter detection the moment they hit the ground, no waiting for the next cycle
description=[*] [b]Three animal limits[/b]: around each player, server-wide total, and per farm — each configurable and overridable per species (e.g. "rat=20, chicken=80")
description=[*] [b]Farm-safe[/b]: penned animals are never cleaned by default; named, led, carried or hooked animals are always protected; "gentle mode" only stops breeding and kills nothing
description=[*] [b]Warned before cleanup[/b]: red text over the player + chat message + sound; green notice when done
description=[*] [b]See who dumped it[/b]: hover an item to see the last dropper and last mover, with time
description=[*] [b]Cleanup log[/b]: every warning and cleanup written to its own log file for admins to audit (`Zomboid\Logs\<start time>_MinidoracatCleanerFor42.txt`)
description=[*] [b]List manager[/b]: right-click in game, search by name or item ID, tick to add in bulk; admins can apply to sandbox with one click, effective immediately
description=[*] [b]Sandbox options[/b]: 24 settings in "General / Items / Animals" tabs, each explained; master switches for items and animals, so you can turn everything off and keep only manual deletion
description=[*] [b]Singleplayer & multiplayer[/b]; languages: Traditional Chinese / Simplified Chinese / English / Japanese
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]⚙️ Defaults[/h2]
description=Works out of the box — "normal play almost never triggers it, malicious dumping is stopped immediately":
description=[list]
description=[*] Player-dropped items: 100 of the same kind per chunk (8×8 tiles), 400 per player's scan area
description=[*] Natural / high-tolerance items: 300 per chunk, 2000 per scan area
description=[*] Free-roaming animals: 50 per group; penned animals and breeding not managed by default
description=[*] Default species: rats, mice, rabbits, chickens
description=[/list]
